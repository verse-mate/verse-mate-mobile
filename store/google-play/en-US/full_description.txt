The Bible was meant to be understood, not just read.

VerseMate helps you go deeper into Scripture with clear explanations for every chapter and verse. Whether you are new to the Bible or have studied it for years, VerseMate makes understanding God's Word simple and accessible.

★ UNDERSTAND AS YOU READ
Read a summary of each chapter, then go verse by verse. Discover the historical context, the original meaning and how Scripture applies to your life today, without needing a seminary degree. Tap a verse to open its Verse Insight.

★ READ IN THE NASB 1995 OR THE KING JAMES VERSION
Switch translations at any time, and read in portrait or landscape, with Jesus's words in red if you like.

★ LISTEN TO THE BIBLE
Hear chapters read aloud in the translation you are reading, and download the narration to listen offline where the translation allows it.

★ WALK THROUGH THE LIFE OF JESUS
Follow his life event by event, by kind or by theme, with each passage alongside a summary, commentary and a guided study.

★ SEARCH THE WHOLE BIBLE
Find any word or phrase across the Bible or in a single book, or jump straight to a reference.

★ A VERSE FOR EVERY DAY
Add the home-screen widget or turn on the daily verse notification, and the day's verse comes to you.

★ MAKE IT YOUR OWN
• Bookmark verses to revisit later
• Highlight passages that speak to you
• Add personal notes and reflections
• Look up the meaning of any word

★ EXPLORE BY TOPIC
Discover what the Bible says about the topics that matter to you, from faith and prayer to relationships and purpose.

★ BUILT BY BELIEVERS
VerseMate is created by a global community of believers united by a shared purpose: making Scripture accessible to everyone, everywhere. We believe that when people truly understand the Bible, lives change.
